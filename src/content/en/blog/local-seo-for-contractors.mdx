A homeowner's water heater fails on a Sunday night. They grab their phone, type “plumber near me”, and call one of the first three businesses on the map. If you're not one of them, the job goes to someone else, whatever your reputation. Local SEO for contractors is about **getting into those three spots, in every neighborhood you want to work in**, not just the one around your shop.

**In short:** local SEO for contractors comes down to five things: a Google Business Profile set up as a service-area business with the right primary category, a website with one page per service and per main city, a steady flow of reviews that mention the job and the location, consistent listings on the directories homeowners use, and tracking to see which neighborhoods and searches bring calls. A GeoGrid shows where you rank across your service area.

<Encadre>
- Google ranks you differently in each neighborhood: you can be #1 near your shop and invisible 5 miles away.
- Your primary category on Google is the single most important setting. Pick your main trade, not a generic one.
- One page per service, and per main city, gives Google a reason to show you outside your immediate area.
- Reviews that mention the job and the city help both your rating and your relevance.
</Encadre>

## The searches homeowners use, by trade

Every trade has its own searches, and its own competitors on each one. Build your profile and your pages around the searches homeowners actually type:

| Trade | Typical homeowner searches | Primary category on Google |
|---|---|---|
| Plumbing | plumber near me, emergency plumber [city], water heater repair [city] | Plumber |
| Roofing | roofer near me, roof repair [city], roof replacement [city] | Roofing contractor |
| Electrical | electrician near me, panel upgrade [city], EV charger installation [city] | Electrician |
| HVAC | AC repair near me, furnace repair [city], HVAC company [city] | HVAC contractor |
| Remodeling | kitchen remodel [city], bathroom remodel near me | Remodeler / Kitchen remodeler |
| Painting | house painters near me, exterior painting [city] | Painter |

*Searches given as typical examples: check the exact wording and volumes for your trade and cities with a keyword tool.*

## The bottom line on local SEO for contractors

Local SEO for contractors isn't about ranking in one spot: it's about showing up in the top three across every neighborhood where you want jobs. Get your primary category and service area right, post job photos, build a page per service and per main city, ask for a review after every job, keep your listings consistent, and track calls and rankings by area. Take the quiz above to find your weakest point, then start there.
