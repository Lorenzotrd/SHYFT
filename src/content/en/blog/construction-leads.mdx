## Local ads: leads from the first week

Your Google profile, reviews and website take several months to reach full effect. Google Ads fills that gap, as long as it's set up right:

- **Specific searches** with your trade and your cities: "bathroom remodel Austin", not "construction".
- **A tight area** limited to the towns where you actually want to work.
- **Negative keywords** so you don't pay for useless searches: jobs, salary, training, DIY, free.
- **A landing page** that matches the ad, with your number and a short form.
- **Call and form tracking**, so you know what a lead really costs, and then a signed job.

<Encadre type="astuce">
Start small: one job type, a few cities, a limited budget. Track leads and signed jobs for a month. Then you'll know whether raising the budget pays off, instead of guessing.
</Encadre>

## How to track where your jobs come from

To know what to keep, you need to know where each job came from. Ask every new customer "how did you hear about us?", write it down, and track calls and form fills on your website. After three months, you'll know which channels bring in signed jobs, not just contacts.
