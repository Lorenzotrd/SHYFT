A one-star review, unfair or flat-out made up, sits at the top of your Google Business Profile. You want to know how to remove a Google review. The short answer: **you can't delete it yourself**, but you do have options, and a well-written reply often matters more than the removal itself.

**In short:** a business can't remove a Google review on its own. It can report reviews that break Google's rules (fake engagement, conflict of interest, offensive or off-topic content), track the report in the Reviews Management Tool and appeal if it's rejected. A negative but genuine review won't be removed: it calls for a public reply.

- A steady flow of genuine reviews dilutes a bad one far faster than any removal.

## Why are Google reviews disappearing?

You got new reviews, and a few days later they're gone. In most cases it's **Google's automated filters**, which hide reviews they consider unreliable. Common triggers:

| Situation | Why Google gets suspicious |
|---|---|
| Lots of reviews on one day after months of silence | Looks like an organized campaign |
| Reviews posted from your premises or Wi-Fi | Suggests they were written on site, or by staff |
| Reviews from brand-new accounts with no other activity | Typical fake account profile |
| Reviews given in exchange for a discount or gift | Against Google's rules |
| Reviews from employees or their families | Conflict of interest |

*Common causes we see: Google doesn't publish how its filters work.*

A review can also disappear because the reviewer deleted it or closed their Google account. The fix is the same either way: **ask for reviews regularly, one at a time, from real customers, right after the job**.

## How to respond to a negative review

Your reply isn't just for the reviewer. It's for the dozens of future customers who will read it before they call you. A calm, specific reply often reassures more than a perfect rating.

Illustrative reply to a genuine negative review:

> Hi Mike, thanks for taking the time to write. You're right: our crew showed up two hours late and we should have called you. We've changed how we schedule morning departures so it doesn't happen again. If you'd like, call me directly at (512) XXX-XXXX to talk it through. Tom, owner.

And to a review you believe is fake:

> Hi, we can't find your name or the job you describe in our records. If you are a customer, please call us at (512) XXX-XXXX so we can understand what happened. The team.

The second reply stays factual and accuses no one. It shows readers you take reviews seriously while your report is being processed.

## The best defense: steady, genuine reviews

A negative review weighs a lot when it sits among a handful of old ones. It weighs much less among recent, detailed reviews. The simplest way to get them: a text sent right after a job well done, with a direct link to your review form.

## Key takeaways on removing a Google review

You can't remove a Google review yourself, but you're not without options. Report reviews that break a specific rule, track them in the Reviews Management Tool and appeal when needed. Reply to everything else, calmly and briefly. Above all, ask every satisfied customer for a review: that steady flow is what protects your rating over time.
