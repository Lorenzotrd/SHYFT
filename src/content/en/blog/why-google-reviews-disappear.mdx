You had 48 Google reviews last week. This morning you have 41. Nobody told you, and the missing reviews were all positive. Good news: **in most cases, this isn't a penalty or an attack**. It's Google's filters doing their job. You still need to know which one kicked in, so it doesn't happen again.

**In short:** Google reviews disappear mostly because Google's spam filters flag them as unreliable (reviews posted in bursts, left from your location, brand-new accounts, reviews given for a reward). Other causes: the reviewer deleted their review or account, the profile was edited, merged or suspended, or a temporary glitch. To keep your reviews, ask for them steadily, one customer at a time, from the customer's own phone, at home.

## What triggers Google's review filters

Google doesn't publish how its filters work. But a few situations come up again and again when reviews vanish:

| Situation | Why Google gets suspicious |
|---|---|
| 10 reviews in two days after three quiet months | Looks like an organized or paid campaign |
| Reviews left on your Wi-Fi or at the counter | Suggests they were written on site, maybe by staff |
| Brand-new accounts with no photo or other reviews | Typical fake-account profile |
| Reviews in exchange for a discount, gift or giveaway | Against Google's policies |
| Reviews from employees, partners or their family | Conflict of interest |
| Very short, near-identical reviews | Sign of reviews written in bulk |
| A link or phone number in the review | Not allowed in reviews |

*Common causes we see: Google doesn't publish how its filters work.*

<Encadre type="attention">
When Google detects fake reviews on a profile, it can go further than removing them: temporarily block new reviews, or show a warning on your profile. Never buy reviews: in the US, the FTC's rule on consumer reviews also prohibits fake and paid reviews, with penalties for businesses.
</Encadre>

## The bottom line on disappearing Google reviews

When Google reviews disappear, it's usually Google's spam filter flagging some of them as unreliable. Check in a private window, ask a reviewer whether they still see their review, check your profile's status, then contact support if nothing explains it. Going forward, one rule: ask for reviews steadily, one real customer at a time, from their own phone. That steady flow protects your rating and your spot on Google Maps.
